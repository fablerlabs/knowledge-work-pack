## Prompts — paste into Claude, fill the blanks, send

| Prompt | Use it when… |
|--------|--------------|
| [`prompts/meeting-notes-to-actions.md`](prompts/meeting-notes-to-actions.md) | you have raw notes or a transcript and need clean decisions, owned action items, and open questions. |
| [`prompts/weekly-update.md`](prompts/weekly-update.md) | it's Friday and you need to turn a week's brain-dump into a crisp status update. |
| [`prompts/decision-memo.md`](prompts/decision-memo.md) | you're weighing "should we do X or Y?" and need a one-pager that forces a real recommendation. |
| [`prompts/inbox-triage-email-draft.md`](prompts/inbox-triage-email-draft.md) | you're staring at a full inbox, or need to draft one reply that sounds like you. |
| [`prompts/summarize-long-doc.md`](prompts/summarize-long-doc.md) | you must extract the signal from a long report, contract, thread, or spec — with page anchors you can verify. |
| [`prompts/competitive-brief.md`](prompts/competitive-brief.md) | you have scattered notes on a competitor or market and want a strategy-ready brief with no invented facts. |
| [`prompts/project-kickoff-brief.md`](prompts/project-kickoff-brief.md) | you're starting something and need everyone aligned on goal, non-goals, and what success looks like. |
| [`prompts/interview-synthesis.md`](prompts/interview-synthesis.md) | you have a pile of user-interview or feedback notes and need honest themes backed by real quotes. |
| [`prompts/prd-drafter.md`](prompts/prd-drafter.md) | you have a rough "we should build X" and need a scoped spec with a real problem statement and explicit non-goals. |
| [`prompts/status-rollup.md`](prompts/status-rollup.md) | you have many people's or teams' updates and need to roll them into one exec summary that leads with what's off-track. |
| [`prompts/feedback-to-themes.md`](prompts/feedback-to-themes.md) | you have a high-volume dump of reviews, support tickets, or NPS verbatims and need prioritized themes with honest counts. |
| [`prompts/sop-writer.md`](prompts/sop-writer.md) | a process lives only in your head and you need a repeatable SOP someone else can follow without asking you. |
| [`prompts/meeting-prep-brief.md`](prompts/meeting-prep-brief.md) | you have a meeting soon and want a one-screen brief: your objective, the room, and the objections you'll face — with answers. |
| [`prompts/rewrite-for-audience.md`](prompts/rewrite-for-audience.md) | you've written something and need it retargeted for a specific reader — right tone and length, same meaning. |
